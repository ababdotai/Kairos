# Reply to the user

Thanks for pointing this out — a timestamp alone isn't enough when several lectures are called “Introduction.” We've updated Kairos to keep the course, lesson and timestamp together, with a Replay button that opens the saved lesson and jumps to the recorded second once the player is ready. We've also improved Coursera with an optional notes drawer that leaves the video layout untouched. These changes are in v1.0.8; the Chrome Web Store update will follow after submission and review. I'd love to hear whether replay works reliably in your course.
