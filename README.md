## 💭 Your attention comes first. Your ideas remain yours.

A good thought shouldn’t be interrupted by switching apps, pasting screenshots or tidying up text. Kairos keeps your notes where inspiration happens, with AI quietly helping while you lead the thinking.

- 🫧 **Stay in flow.** Capture a thought without leaving the video.
- 🧠 **Build on your thinking.** AI uses the frame and context to organize notes while preserving your original words.
- ⏱️ **Connect the moments.** A timeline links your insights to their source, so revisiting doesn’t mean guessing where to scrub.
- 🔐 **Keep ownership.** Notes stay on your device, with AI available when you choose.

**Watch more efficiently. Understand more deeply. Learn more actively.**

## ✨ From a fleeting thought to a lasting note

- **✍️ Catch the thought.** Type a few words or drag to capture part of the frame. Save your note and get straight back to watching.
- **🪄 Let AI lend a hand.** Optional AI refinement turns rough keywords into clearer notes using the frame and available captions. Your original words stay alongside the result in the dashboard.
- **⏱️ Return to the moment.** Glowing timeline markers take you back to each note. Notes reappear during playback; hover to keep reading.
- **🫧 Keep the video in view.** A movable glass island fades into the background when you leave it. Expand it when you need more room.
- **📚 Make your notes useful.** Browse vertical or zigzag timelines, create AI summaries, export Markdown, or turn selected notes into a webpage you can download as HTML.

## 🎬 Bring Kairos along

**YouTube · Bilibili** — supported.

**Coursera · Udemy · TED** — experimental support; authenticated course playback still needs live verification.

Works on supported playback pages, not homepage previews. Cross-origin embedded players and DRM-protected frame capture are not supported.

## 🚀 Three steps to your first note

1. **Install** from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/kairos-your-ai-video-note/idhcehbbnfdklhbondmbpplpcdbnodfp).
2. **Open a video and enable Kairos.** Start typing, or drag over the video to capture a region.
3. **Press Enter to save.** Want AI help? Connect your own AI provider in Settings, then turn on refinement.

Manual notes work without an API key. The extension is free; optional AI usage is billed by your provider.

## 🔐 Your notes, your choice

Notes and screenshots stay on your device by default. AI features send the relevant note, frame and/or caption context to your chosen provider. API keys and preferences use Chrome sync storage.

Share-page HTML can be previewed and downloaded locally. Publishing public links requires a [configured hosting service](doc/share-pages-setup.md) and an explicit publish action; it is not enabled by default.
